Your Boring Job Is Now My Problem

Welcome. You did it.
You found something boring, repetitive, or annoying enough to hand it off to someone else.

Thanks for trusting me with it. Here's everything you need to know about how we'll work together.

A. What I Can and Can't Do

I'm happy to take on boring, repetitive online work that I can reasonably complete remotely.

Examples include:

  • Data entry and organization
  • Research
  • Transcription
  • Translation
  • Document formatting
  • Content briefs/summaries
  • Spreadsheet cleanup
  • Simple web app development
  • Other repetitive digital tasks

If you're unsure whether your task qualifies, ask me.

I won't accept work that is illegal, fraudulent, deceptive, unsafe, or requires access to accounts or information that I shouldn't have.

B. How We’ll Work Together

  • 1. Send the work through any of my contact options;
    • Instagram DM
    • WhatsApp
    • Email

    Pick whichever medium you're most comfortable with, and send me the work description.

    Instagram WhatsApp Email
  • 2. I’ll reply and confirm to you right away whether the job is something I can do. From there, we'll conclude on how many pennies I'll get for the job.

    My service is obviously very specific to what you want, so I can't spell out a definite upfront price here. It can range from $5 for low effort stuff like document formatting, to around $50 for stuff like web app development or advanced spreadsheets.

  • 3. Once that's done, you'll send me everything I need to get started.

    You can send files, screenshots, links, instructions, examples, references, or anything else relevant.

    The golden rule here is to tell me exactly what "done" looks like.

    If you have a specific format, deadline, naming convention, tone, or other requirement, tell me upfront.

    The clearer the instructions, the less back-and-forth we'll need.

  • 4. Once I receive your materials, I'll review them and confirm:
    • That I have everything I need
    • What exactly I'll be delivering
    • When you can expect it

    If something is unclear or missing, I'll ask before starting.

  • 5. I'll notify you once the project is completed, and send you the details to make payment. Once payment is confirmed, your completed work will be sent through the agreed communication channel, or delivered using a download link.

    Standard turnaround: 48 hours.

    Some jobs will be completed sooner. Larger or complicated jobs may take longer, in which case I'll tell you before I begin.

  • 6. If I've misunderstood your instructions, or missed something that was part of your original request, I'll fix it.

    Your job should come back exactly the way you expected it to.

    Therefore, one reasonable revision is included where appropriate. An appropriate revision means adjusting the original job.

    It does not mean adding an entirely new job after delivery.

    For example:

    An appropriate revision request is something like:

    "Can you change the document font to match the example I originally provided?"

    Or

    "Can you make the dark mode be the default theme of the app"

    And an inappropriate one is like:

    "Actually, can you also turn the whole YouTube transcript into a beautiful 30-page PDF I can sell on the internet?"

    If you've got something new, just ask me. I'll tell you what how many extra coins I’ll take for that new task, and get right back to work!

C. Communication

You don't need to schedule a call or meeting with me.

Just send me the task, give me the instructions, and I'll handle it asynchronously.

For normal communication (say extra context, examples, or requests), use the channel you sent the job description through.

I'll generally respond within 10-60 minutes regardless of what day it is, unless it's nighttime.

D. A Small Request From Me

Please don't send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

If I need access to something, we'll figure out the safest way to provide it.

And if your task contains confidential information from the get-go, give me a heads up before sending anything to me.
