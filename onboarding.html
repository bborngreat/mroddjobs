Your Boring Job Is Now My Problem

Welcome! You did it.

You found something boring, repetitive, or annoying enough to hand it off to someone else. That someone is me.

Thanks for trusting me with it. Here's everything you need to know about how we'll work together.

A. What I Can And Can't Do

I'm happy to take on boring, repetitive online work that I can complete remotely.

Examples include:

  • Data entry and organization
  • Research
  • Transcription
  • Translation
  • Document formatting
  • Content summaries
  • Spreadsheet cleanup
  • Simple web app development
  • Other repetitive digital tasks

Note: I won't accept work that is illegal, fraudulent, deceptive, unsafe, or requires access to accounts or information that I shouldn't have.

I respect your privacy, and I'm not going to force you to trust me simply because I sound innocent or harmless.

I won't ever request access to your password, account logins, banking credentials, credit card information, 2-Factor Authentication codes, or government ID. Any task that would mandate access to such information will be politely declined.

If you're unsure whether your task still qualifies, don't go just yet. Just ask me, and I'll promptly tell you if it does or not.

B. How We’ll Work Together

  • 1. Submission:

    There's a big, yellow "Submit your task" button at the end of this page. Click it, and fill out a short form which gives me a good overview of the work you'd like to get off your plate.

  • 2. Confirmation & Quote:

    I’ll reach out and confirm to you right away whether the job is something I can do (based on complexity and data security).

    From there, we'll conclude on how many pennies I'll get for the job.

    My service is obviously very specific to what you want, so I can't spell out a definite upfront price here. It can range from $5 for tasks like document formatting, to around $30 or more for projects like web app development.

    I don't charge hourly, so you won't have to worry about exaggerated working hours or outrageous hourly rates. Your project will be priced strictly based on its complexity, not the time I take to do it.

  • 3. Agreement & Context Provision:

    Once we agree on a fair price, you'll send me the files, screenshots, links, instructions, examples, features, and anything else relevant I need to get started.

    Also, if you have a specific format, deadline, naming convention, tone, or other requirement, here's where you'll tell me.

    The clearer the instructions, the less back-and-forth we'll need later on.

  • 4. Work & Review:

    I'll get to work immediately I have everything I need. We won't need to have any calls or meetings.

    Once I complete the project, I'll send you a link to let you preview the results. From that review, you'll be given one reasonable revision.

    If I've misunderstood your instructions, or missed something that was part of your original request, I'll fix it.

    Important Note: A revision means adjusting the original job. It does not mean adding an entirely new job after delivery.

    For example:

    An appropriate revision request is something like:

    “Can you change the document font to match the example I originally provided?”

    Or

    “Can you make the dark mode be the default theme of the app”

    And an inappropriate one is like:

    “Actually, can you also turn the whole YouTube transcript into a beautiful 30-page PDF I can sell on the internet?”

    Or

    “Can you translate the content into French as well in addition to the Spanish version?”

    If you've got something new, just ask me. I'll tell you how many extra coins that new task will cost, and get right back to work!

  • 5. Payment & Delivery:

    After the appropriate revisions (or extra projects) are complete and you are satisfied with the preview(s), I'll send you the details to make payment.

    And once payment is confirmed, your full, completed work will be sent through our agreed communication channel, or delivered using a download link.

  • 6. Rating:

    If you had an amazing experience, (and you want to), you can give me a fire review directly via text or email, or even record a video testimonial.

    To be clear, you're not obligated to. I may ask for it after the project is delivered, and you can simply decline. Or you can ask that your review be displayed anonymously (without your name or identity).

  • 7. Next Steps:

    From there, you can simply say "okay, bye" and block me. You can send me another job you've got so we run it all back again. Or we can become good friends and live happily ever after!

    You don't have to be in any sort of relationship or contact with me if you don't want to. So whatever you choose, there'll be no hard feelings.

C. A Small Request From Me

Please don't send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

I am not a trustworthy saint, and I will not pretend to be. My device can get hacked or stolen. I can download a virus from the web. And I can be tracked and surveilled as a result of using the internet.

Therefore, do not give me any information that you wouldn't scream out loud in public, without first giving me a heads up about it.

If I need access to something, we'll figure out the safest way to provide it, like a simple screenshot of the relevant context from your account, or simply typing out what I need to me via text.

And if your task contains confidential information from the get-go, give me a heads up before sending anything to me.

For more information on this, and several other important privacy concerns, please check out my privacy policy
